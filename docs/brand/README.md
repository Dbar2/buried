# Brand direction references

These images are concept art, made with an image model on Nov 13, 2026 to settle the look before
building it. They are mood and layout targets, not screenshots. The maps, street names and
buildings drawn in them are invented or redrawn and are not evidence; nothing from them ships in
the app. Their props (a brass lamp, books) are not part of the product, and their ghosts have
windows and chimneys that no source gives; Buried's ghosts are plain volumes with ink edges.

| File | What it settles |
|---|---|
| `concept-a-landing.jpg` | Search first. A lit map sheet under a foggy block, ink ghosts rising. |
| `concept-b-place-page.jpg` | The place page: the dig on the left, a paper report on the right, a core sample of real crops per era, plain sentences. |
| `concept-c-studio.jpg` | The Studio: the scan dimmed so traced outlines read, a dense calm side panel, one primary action. |
| `concept-d-identity.jpg` | Wordmark, the core-sample mark, palette, type, and the three confidence line styles. |
| `concept-h-theatre.jpg` | The landing (chosen): the sheet on a dark table lit from the upper left, "Something used to stand here.", one lantern "Raise the past" button, paper tags on strings. |
| `concept-f-raised.jpg` | After Raise: ghosts on the sheet, the selected find in lantern, a leader line to the catalogue card at the right, find tags along the top, the three-stop era ruler. |
| `concept-g-phone.jpg` | The phone: scene above with the Lantern lens and the ruler, the catalogue card as a bottom sheet. |
| `concept-e-landing.jpg` | A calmer landing. Not chosen. |

Concepts a to d are superseded for the landing and the place page layout (session 037); c still
guides the Studio.

The decisions taken from them are written into `.kiro/steering/design.md` and
`.kiro/steering/product.md`; those files, not these images, are what the build follows.
