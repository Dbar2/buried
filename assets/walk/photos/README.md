Two things these photographs change:

- The City's heritage survey dates the bank at 541 Queen Street East to 1912, and the October 1911
  photograph shows no bank at the corner. Our plan is the 1911 edition **revised to 1914**, which is
  why it draws the bank. The walk shows the block as that revised plan draws it, so its date is
  "about 1914", not 1911.
- The 1911 photograph shows a Carhartt sign on the roof, not painted on the wall.
