# Imagined views (how they were made)

The pictures in `web/public/lotcheck/imagined/` were made on Nov 25, 2026 with an image model.
Input for each: a north-up crop of the plan around the site, and a period photograph or the 1939
aerial photograph for tone. Each top-down view was overlaid with the plan's outlines and checked
by eye; one (the knitting mill) did not match its plan and was dropped. Every picture has the word
IMAGINED set into its lower left corner by `tools/publish_imagined_views.py`. The full-size
originals are kept off the repository. They are illustration, not evidence.
